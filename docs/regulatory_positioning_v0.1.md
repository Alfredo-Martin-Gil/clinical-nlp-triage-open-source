# Exploratory Regulatory Considerations — v0.1

## Document status

This document records preliminary regulatory questions for a research
prototype. It is not a regulatory strategy, classification decision, conformity
assessment, submission plan, or statement of compliance.

The current repository has no authorized clinical intended use, no patient use,
no deployment, and no claim of clinical safety or effectiveness.

## Current position

At its current stage, the repository is presented only as open-source research
and educational software evaluated on synthetic data. It must not be marketed,
represented, or used as a medical device or clinical decision-support product.

No claim is made that the project is:

- compliant with ANMAT or Health Canada requirements;
- classified under a specific medical-device class;
- eligible or ready for a submission;
- aligned with a complete SaMD clinical-evaluation pathway;
- developed under a certified QMS;
- suitable for clinical investigation or deployment.

## Why classification is premature

Regulatory classification depends on jurisdiction-specific intended use,
claims, users, population, environment, degree of automation, and consequences
of erroneous output. Those elements are deliberately not fixed for v0.x.

The current internal labels and `recommended_action` field resemble clinical
risk communication, while the synthetic benchmark shows major failure modes.
This combination requires technical and clinical remediation before a credible
future intended-use discussion.

## Questions for future qualified review

If the project later evolves beyond research, qualified regulatory and legal
review would need to determine:

1. whether the proposed software falls within the medical-device definition in
   each jurisdiction;
2. how intended use and claims affect classification;
3. what clinical evidence, software lifecycle, risk-management, cybersecurity,
   usability, privacy, and post-market obligations apply;
4. whether public open-source distribution changes responsibilities or risk
   controls;
5. whether the proposed environment and user population make the risk
   unacceptable.

ANMAT and Health Canada may be future jurisdictions of interest. Mentioning
them does not establish alignment, readiness, or a Canadian/Argentine project.

## Governance rule

No future document may state or imply regulatory alignment, certification
readiness, class, clearance, approval, compliance, or submission intent without:

- a defined version and intended use;
- primary-source regulatory review current to the relevant date;
- qualified interpretation;
- documented evidence and accountable approval.

## Present disclaimer

No real-world patient use, clinical pilot, deployment, or regulatory submission
is authorized or supported by this repository version.
